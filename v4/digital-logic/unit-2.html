Combinational circuits

Outputs depend on current inputs after propagation delay. Design from a truth table, simplify each output, then implement the expressions with gates. Sequential circuits also depend on stored state.

CircuitInputsOutputs
Half adderA, BS=A⊕B, C=AB
Full adderA, B, carry-in CᵢS=A⊕B⊕Cᵢ, Cₒ=AB+Cᵢ(A⊕B)
Half subtractorA, BD=A⊕B, borrow=¬A·B
Full subtractorA, B, borrow-in BᵢD=A⊕B⊕Bᵢ, Bₒ=¬A·B+¬(A⊕B)·Bᵢ

For 0111 + 0001, the carry travels through the three low bits before the high bit becomes 1. A chain of full adders is therefore called a ripple-carry adder. For an adder/subtractor, XOR each B bit with SUB and set the first carry-in to SUB. SUB=0 computes A+B and SUB=1 computes A+¬B+1.

Sequential circuits and flip-flops

A latch responds while its enable level is active. An edge-triggered flip-flop samples around the chosen clock edge. Meet setup and hold time around that edge and allow clock-to-Q delay before the new output settles. Violations can cause metastability, which is why an asynchronous single-bit input normally crosses into a clock domain through a synchronizer.

TypeNext state Q⁺ at the active edge
SR, active-high conventionSR=00 hold, 01 reset, 10 set, 11 forbidden.
DQ⁺=D.
JKJK=00 hold, 01 reset, 10 set, 11 toggle. Q⁺=J¬Q+¬KQ.
TT=0 hold, T=1 toggle. Q⁺=T⊕Q.

To build a T flip-flop from D, feed D=T⊕Q. For a JK device, tie J and K to T. In level-sensitive JK implementations with J=K=1, a long active clock pulse can permit repeated toggles. Edge-triggered operation avoids multiple updates within the same edge.

Four-bit binary counters

Both up counters count 0000 through 1111 and then wrap to 0000. In a synchronous design every flip-flop shares the external clock. With T flip-flops, T₀=1, T₁=Q₀, T₂=Q₁Q₀ and T₃=Q₂Q₁Q₀. A bit toggles if all lower bits were 1 just before the edge.

In the ripple design, the transition 0111 → 1000 can briefly pass through 0110, 0100 and 0000. Decode outputs only after they settle or register the decoded result with suitable timing. In the synchronous version clock-to-Q skew can still affect combinational decoding, but there is no stage-by-stage clock ripple.

Practice

What does a full adder output for A=B=Cᵢ=1?

S=1 and Cₒ=1, since decimal 3 is binary 11.

Which synchronous counter bits toggle from 1011?

Q₀, Q₁ and Q₂ toggle. Q₃ holds because Q₂ was 0. The next word is 1100.

What are D and Q⁺ if a D-based T flip-flop has T=1 and Q=0?

D=1⊕0=1, so Q⁺ becomes 1 on the active edge.
